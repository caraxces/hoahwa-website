Modifications in game: City.glb is the supplied file, unchanged. The runtime hides the
included ground plane and sky sphere, scales the cluster by 48.35 and places it nine
times (layout.json) to form the Neon Night City. surface.bin / surface.json are a derived
2 m collision field for those placements. Lighting, streets, lamps, stations, wind rings
and billboards are HoaHwa additions.
